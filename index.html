How to get a good scan
  • Turn on the lights. Depth and tracking need light and texture.
  • Start by moving the phone gently side to side until the status says Capturing.
  • Walk slowly. Sweep each wall from floor to ceiling, staying 0.5–3 m away.
  • Pass through doorways slowly and look back into the room you left, so rooms link up.
  • Glass, mirrors, glossy and plain white surfaces scan poorly.
  • End the scan back where you started. Tracking drifts over long paths, so do big houses in a few scans.

Needs Chrome on an Android phone with Google Play Services for AR and Depth API support. Everything runs on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
